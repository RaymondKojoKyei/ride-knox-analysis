# Memo to the Director of Operations

## Bottom line
Fall ridership held up better than last year once Day Pass is counted, but vendor delivery quality remains a contract risk. Member trips were up 11.6% in August (19,252 versus 17,249) and 11.2% in September (18,660 versus 16,777). Casual-only trips fell 14.8% in August (6,417 versus 7,534) and 13.4% in September (5,480 versus 6,331), while casual plus Day Pass rose 39.5% in August (10,508 versus 7,534) and 39.3% in September (8,820 versus 6,331).

## Evidence
July was usable after 120 duplicate copies and 40 trips with missing start times were accounted for. August was usable after repairing 45 rows with an extra comma in one station name. The first September delivery used an unapproved field order and was not used; its corrected resend was usable. October was truncated and produced no usable monthly result. Cargo use rose from 350 downtown trips in July to 520 in August and 780 in September. Campus cargo activity first appears on September 15 at S07 and S08, then S06 later that day; no usable data show S09 or S10 joining.

## Interpretation
The summer recovery appears to have continued into early fall, especially for members and when Day Pass riders are included. The cargo pilot expanded beyond downtown.

## Limitations
October cannot support a monthly conclusion. July includes June backlog, so delivery month is not travel month. Forty July trips lack start times. The 2025 comparison has no separate Day Pass category, so the combined comparison uses casual riders as the denominator.

## Recommendation
For January renewal, require the contracted field order, complete monthly delivery, explicit resend/version labeling, and quality reporting. Continue measuring cargo demand by station and confirm whether S09 and S10 are intended expansion sites.
