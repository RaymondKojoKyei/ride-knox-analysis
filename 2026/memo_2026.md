# Memo: Day Pass & Capacity - Six-Month Verdict

**To:** Director of Operations, Ride Knox
**From:** Data Analysis Team
**Date:** July 2026
**Re:** Did the 2026 changes work?

**Bottom line:** The Day Pass and the campus capacity investments are working;
one 2025 station is not.

**Evidence.** Counting casual riders alone, ridership is still down 18% in
June 2026 versus June 2025 (11,344 vs. 13,816 trips). Counting casual plus the
new day-pass riders, non-member trips passed the 2025 baseline in May (+7%)
and reached +12% in June (15,461 vs. 13,816). Member trips grew about 10%
every month. On capacity: pressure at Hodges Library and the Student Union
fell 31% (from ~94 and ~91 starts per dock per month to ~65 and ~62) after the
dock expansions and the new Cumberland & 22nd station, which debuted third
busiest system-wide (~100 starts per dock per month over its four months).
Bearden doubled its per-dock use; Sequoyah Hills stayed flat at the system low.

**Interpretation (labeled as such):** day-pass riders take long, weekend-heavy
trips like the casual riders we lost - this looks like recovery of that
segment, not members trading down.

**Limitations:** six months of data; no controlled comparison (spring weather
and novelty are uncontrolled); ~800 early-2026 trips lack end times due to an
app bug and are excluded from duration figures.

**Recommendation:** keep the Day Pass; monitor fall 2026 before judging it
fully; begin relocation planning for Sequoyah Hills.
