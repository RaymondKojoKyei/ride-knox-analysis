# Ride Knox 2026 Public Update

**To:** Director of Operations, Ride Knox  
**Cc:** Marketing Lead  
**Subject:** Updated public Ride Knox analysis

The updated Ride Knox public analysis is now ready for review. The page brings the 2025 baseline and the first six months of 2026 together so the main changes in ridership and station use can be seen in one place.

The clearest 2026 result is that the Day Pass appears to be helping recover non-member trips. Casual ridership alone remained below June 2025, but when Day Pass trips are included, non-member trips were 12% above the June 2025 level. The page also shows that pressure at Hodges Library and the Student Union fell by about 31% after capacity changes.

Compared with the earlier board version, the public page now includes the 2026 results, updated charts, a two-year comparison, and clearer explanations of what the changes mean operationally.

One important caveat is that the 2026 results cover only six months, so seasonal conditions and other uncontrolled factors may affect the comparison.

Start with the 2026 ridership recovery chart, then review the 2026 memo for the supporting details.
